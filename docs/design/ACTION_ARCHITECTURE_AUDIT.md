# Action architecture and current catalog audit

Code audit for the integrated Forest, action, lead, Journal, crafting, and equipment iteration. The source of truth is `src/dimensional_sim/world/catalog.py` (definitions), `actions.py` (eligibility), `encounters.py` (placement), `autopilot/` (life cycle), and `equipment.py` (slots). This records implemented rules; it does not claim that every gated action occurs frequently in ordinary play. Seeded frequency and balance results belong in `BALANCING.md` after simulation.

## Availability contract

```text
biome -> generated region -> local action pool
      -> known (unlock, knowledge, recipe)
      -> progression requirements and Journal filter
      -> weighted bucket -> deterministic spot roll
      -> spawn window/pity admission -> reachable live spot
      -> local context -> completion -> resources, knowledge, recipe, or lead
```

`Context` in `actions.py` carries biome and pinned region, owned unlocks, knowledge, recipes, action/item completion counts, mastery, Journal settings, inventory, equipment, and trigger. `known()` is **permission**, not a spawn. `bucket_reasons()` gives progression, region, and Journal blockers; `context_reasons()` gives immediate self-action blockers such as ingredients, hunger, and already carried gear. `explain()` reports these separately along with weight, roll state, window admission, and availability when a caller supplies concrete state. `eligible` remains the legacy combined bucket-and-context result. A rolled spot reports `ROLLED` until window admission is known, `NOT_ADMITTED` if rejected, and `AVAILABLE` only once admitted and context eligible. A positive bucket weight never proves a spot exists.

World spots use `chunk_spots(chunk, life, context=...)`: chunk seed, encounter version, and life determine count, choices, and reachable cells. The bucket uses the current region's action multipliers and earned Journal odds. `Expedition._screen()` enforces per-life at-once caps and food/enemy pity; a rolled spot can therefore be rejected. Completing a spot consumes that instance for the life. Permanent unlocks permit future rolls but never create permanent world buttons. Self recipes share eligibility rules but are chosen by the need policy when their materials and conditions hold; they do not require terrain spawning. After-location reflection/prayer uses its own weighted self bucket. Temporary leads are generated by action outcomes and placed by `lead_spot()` in the source chunk; they never join the global spot pool.

Journal control is earned through ash-purchased mastery of **any spot** action: level 2 permits enabling/disabling it for future rolls, level 3 permits favoring (150%) or suppressing (50%) its weight. `economy.MASTERABLE` includes all 13 spot actions, including unwindowed branches and tracks. Suppression keeps a positive weight; disabling removes it from future buckets. Neither state guarantees a roll or removes an already-created lead. Mastery also widens authored spawn windows where those exist. The Journal's lifetime action/item counts and achievements remain separate from its roll controls.

The catalog's `roles` are descriptive classifications. A base spot still requires a region-valid bucket roll, admission, and reachability. The two deer leads are contextual opportunities and have no permanent spot weight; their weight field is schema data only. Self need actions are priority ordered from catalog data, so their weight is not used for selection. The engine uses item definitions to distinguish food, material, and gear; wearing is represented by explicit `weapon`/`body` slots, while inventory possession alone is not an equipped benefit.

## Integrated chains and regions

```text
Deep Woods / Still Glade -> animal_tracks spot -> deer_sign knowledge
    -> 30%: temporary follow_deer_tracks (90 s) -> follow -> 60%: temporary hunt_deer (60 s)
    -> hunt -> venison

Old Road / Deep Woods / Thicket -> fallen_branches spot -> 5 completions
    -> primitive_crafting knowledge -> 10 completions -> walking_staff recipe -> 3 sticks
    -> craft_staff self action -> walking_staff inventory item + weapon slot
```

`REGIONS` defines Old Road, Deep Woods, Bramble Thicket, and Still Glade. A 3×3-chunk region cell is chosen from the world seed; the origin cell is Old Road. Each region specifies canopy density, brush density, landmark grammar, tint, and action weight multipliers. Generation composes those parameters into terrain/props, while action buckets use the pinned region definition from the world repository. Branches cannot roll in Still Glade; tracks can roll only in Deep Woods or Still Glade. This connects both chains to exploration. Different seeds can produce different non-origin arrangements; the same seed and pinned catalog reproduce them. See `regions.py`, `generation.py`, `open_terrain.py`, and `repository.py` for the pipeline.

## Persistence and migration

Expedition schema 8 (world schema 2) stores current life/world state separately from persistent progression. `knowledge`, `recipes`, unlocks, ash-purchased mastery, Journal roll settings and lifetime counts, currencies, dimensional XP, and best depth survive death and save/load. Inventory, equipped slots, active leads, completed spots, regular XP, vitals, tasks, spawn windows, admission decisions, and pity counters are current-life state: they survive save/load during that life but reset at rebirth. A lead expires at its saved `expires_ms`, resolves on completion, and is cleared on death. The world's generated/visited state and region catalog live in the exploration repository save; its region catalog is pinned for deterministic replay. The schema-7 upgrade carries legacy earned progression, migrates carried gear into slots, initializes newly added knowledge/recipe/Journal controls empty, clears affected current-life spot plans, and rerolls under encounter version 7. Upgrades from schemas 6-8 live in `autopilot/saves.py` (`MIGRATIONS`); since 2026-10-07 saves older than schema 6 are rejected.

## Follow-up decisions (2026-10-06)

- **Deer chain tuning.** With the first numbers (55% lead, guaranteed hunt, 1-2 venison) the deer chain fed
  ~13 hunts per life and first lives lasted ~44 min (8 seeds x 60 min). It now gives a 30% follow lead, a 60%
  hunt lead and 1 venison; lives returned to ~18 min, the chain still completes on most seeds.
- **Crafting gates** moved from 3/5 to 5/10 branch completions so the staff is a mid-first-life milestone
  (minute 3-6 on seeds 1-8), not an opening move.
- **Retreat is not free.** A boar that reaches an avatar fleeing to camp gores it in passing (it does not stop it).
  Before this, boars could never kill an avatar that chose to flee.
- **Old worlds.** Generator-v1 (homogeneous) worlds stay byte-identical mid-life, and are regenerated from the
  same seed with region-shaped generator v2 at the next rebirth (`Expedition._current_generator_world`).
- **Observability.** `actions.explain()` reports a single lifecycle `state`; `cli inspect` explains region
  provenance, generation parameters and terrain; `cli regions` prints the layout; the debug overlay lists states,
  knowledge, recipes and leads.
- **Spot cache key** now includes every roll input (mastery behind Journal choices and any spot-action
  requirement), so a reloaded save re-rolls exactly what a live run had cached.
