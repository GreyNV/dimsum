# Open design decisions

These are intentionally unresolved until the first prototype produces useful evidence.

- The exact vitality decay and recovery curve.
- The exact formula for action speed and discipline XP requirements.
- Whether softcaps affect XP, speed, vitality efficiency, encounter progress, or separate configured channels.
- The exact rarity thresholds for journal unlocks and encounter weights.
- Final equipment slot taxonomy/balance; the prototype uses head, body, hands, feet, weapon and charm.
- Summon pity, duplicate conversion, final shard cost and XP/cap/matrix tuning; current pulls retain distinct instances and share a configured cost.
- The exact offline efficiency curve for automatic lives and passive clone output.
- What dimensional shift does to dimensional experience and other persistent state.
- World survival tuning: hunger/health rates, danger growth per ring (x1.5), boar
  HP, the fight/retreat thresholds and food values (docs/technical/world-autopilot.md).
- What crafting and binding at the anchor camp consume and produce.
- How world lives relate to idle lives (shared XP, vitality, shards, journal).
- Blessing power (from prayer): what it buys, and whether gods differ.
- Spawn window tuning: food is capped, but boars still cause every death in samples.
- Bandit ambush: replace the placeholder memory lines with the original text from the earlier game.
